# Musical Insights Pro — Marketing & Product Overview

## What It Is

Musical Insights Pro is a browser-based, visual-first music theory and guitar
practice platform. It replaces the mental math of "what scale fits this
chord?" or "where are the chord tones on the neck?" with instant, color-coded,
interactive diagrams that update live as you change key, scale, chord, or
position. It's not a tab site and not a DAW — it's the missing bridge between
knowing theory on paper and actually playing it in real time on the
instrument.

Everything in the app revolves around one shared, always-in-sync state: the
current **root note + scale/mode**. Every panel — the fretboard, the Circle
of Fifths, the chord tools, the recommendation engines, the Song Builder —
reacts instantly to that state, so a player never has to manually reconcile
different views of the same key.

---

## Core Capabilities

### Interactive Fretboard Visualization
A full SVG fretboard (6- or 7-string, any tuning, up to 24 frets) renders
every note of the active scale as a colored dot, with a fixed color per note
name so the ear-to-eye mapping never changes across the whole app. Chord
tones (root/3rd/5th/7th), guide tones (3rd/7th), CAGED shapes, triad
overlays, and pentatonic overlays can all be layered on or stripped away —
including an "Only Chord Tones" mode that hides everything except what you
need to hear.

### Intervals & Chord Tones by Position and Degree
For any key and any scale degree (I, ii, iii, IV, V, vi, vii°), the app shows
exactly which notes are the root, 3rd, 5th, and 7th of that position's triad
or chord — visually overlaid directly on the scale shape you're already
looking at. Instead of memorizing "the third of the ii chord in D major is
F#," you *see* it highlighted inside the scale pattern, in the position your
hand is already in. This is what makes the app distinctive for interval-based
learning rather than rote scale-shape memorization.

### Best Scale & Mode Recommendations
For any key or chord, the app surfaces the compatible scales/modes ranked by
fit, so a player always has a theory-backed answer to "what can I play over
this?" — without needing to work it out by hand.

### Custom & AI-Assisted Chord Progression Tools
Build progressions manually from any scale degree, save/load them, or
generate progression ideas from natural-language mood/genre prompts. Diatonic
chords, common progressions (I–V–vi–IV, ii–V–I, 12-bar blues, etc.), and
fully custom sequences are all one click from playback preview.

### Chord Neighborhood Finder
Pick a chord and the Neighborhood Finder scans every diatonic chord in the
key and finds the **nearest playable voicing** to your current hand
position — ranked by fret-distance and shared/common tones. Feed it a full
chord progression and it maps each chord to a position clustered as close
together as possible, so an entire progression can be played with minimal
hand movement instead of jumping across the neck. Common tones between
adjacent chords are highlighted so smooth voice-leading is visible, not
guessed at.

### Full Song Builder
A dedicated multi-section workspace (`/chord-progression-builder`) for
constructing full songs: drag-and-drop chord and scale/mode blocks onto a
BPM-accurate timeline, overlay the compatible scale for every chord change
or section automatically, preview with real instrument audio (piano, guitar,
bass), score the harmonic tension/resolution of the whole progression, and
save projects to your account. Every chord change carries its own
recommended-scale overlay, so switching sections never breaks the "what do I
play here" answer.

### Hands-Free MIDI Pedal Control
Connect a standard MIDI foot pedal and drive the app without touching a
mouse or keyboard: switch scales, triads, fretboard positions, and
recommended modes with foot switches. Sections can be individually enabled
for pedal control and cycled through live, which is built specifically for
players who need both hands on the instrument — during practice, teaching,
or performance.

### Setlists with Key/Scale Changes
Chain multiple keys, scales, and progressions together into a performance
order, so a full set's worth of harmonic reference material is pre-built and
ready to page through live.

### Harmonization & Compatible Scale Tools
Diatonic harmonization of any key is generated automatically, with
compatible-scale suggestions per chord and mode-relationship insight (which
modes share the most tones with a given chord), so substitutions and
color-tone choices are grounded in real harmonic relationships, not guesswork.

## Why Each User Gets Real Value

### a. Guitar Students (Lessons or Self-Taught)
Most beginners get stuck translating "the scale" into "notes I can actually
use over this chord." Musical Insights Pro removes the guesswork: pick a key,
see the scale, see the chord tones inside it, and see which scales are
recommended for the chord you're on. The Only Chord Tones mode is a built-in
practice filter for targeting chord tones instead of noodling randomly, and
the Neighborhood Finder keeps early progressions in one comfortable hand
position instead of forcing wide jumps a beginner isn't ready for.

### b. Guitar Instructors (Especially Interval-First Teachers)
For instructors who teach the neck by intervals and relationships rather than
memorized scale boxes, this app *is* the whiteboard. Every scale degree's
chord tones are shown as an overlay on the same scale shape, in the same
position, which is exactly how interval-based teaching frames the neck:
"here is where the 3rd of the IV chord lives inside the shape you already
know." It turns an abstract explanation into a shared, live visual a student
can watch change in real time, and the Song Builder lets an instructor
construct example progressions on the spot for a lesson without prep time.

### c. College Music Students
Music theory coursework demands fluency with diatonic harmony, modal
relationships, voice leading, and harmonic function — often faster than
students can internalize by ear or by hand alone. The app's automatic
diatonic harmonization, compatible-mode analysis, and Roman-numeral/position
mapping act as a always-available study reference that mirrors what's taught
in theory class, while the fretboard makes the same concepts tangible on an
instrument rather than staying confined to a staff-paper exercise.

### d. Live Players
Gigging musicians don't have time to think through theory mid-song. The
Circle of Fifths gives an instant visual key reference, the MIDI pedal
control switches scales/positions/modes hands-free while both hands stay on
the guitar, and setlists with pre-built key/scale changes mean the harmonic
map for an entire set is ready before the first downbeat. The Neighborhood
Finder is particularly valuable live: it keeps chord-to-chord movement
physically efficient, reducing missed changes from big neck jumps under
performance pressure.

### e. Improvisers
Improvising well depends on knowing, in the moment, which notes are safe,
which are color tones, and where the chord tones sit relative to the scale
shape currently under the fingers. The app's real-time compatible-scale
recommendations, chord-tone overlays per position, and mode-relationship
data give an improviser instant, trustworthy answers instead of relying on
memorized licks. Combined with hands-free MIDI switching, a soloist can pivot
scale/mode choices mid-phrase as the harmony changes, without breaking
concentration or technique to reach for a screen.

## The General Value Proposition

Musical Insights Pro turns music theory from something you calculate into
something you *see* — mapped directly onto the fretboard, synced across every
tool in the app, and available hands-free when both hands are busy playing.
Whether the goal is learning your first scale, teaching intervals instead of
shapes, passing a theory exam, surviving a live set, or improvising with
confidence, the app answers the same core question instantly and visually:
**"What do I play right now, and where is it on the neck?"**
